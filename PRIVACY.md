# 琴桥隐私说明 / QinBridge Privacy Notice

更新日期 / Updated: 2026-10-07

## 中文

琴桥将曲谱、乐器按键配置和界面偏好保存在本机。软件不设琴桥账户，不向开发者上传你的曲库、按键配置或窗口标题，也没有广告或分析统计 SDK。

你打开「在线曲库」或点击刷新、下载时，软件会从本公开 GitHub 仓库请求曲谱目录和文件。请求不包含你的本地曲库。GitHub 会处理提供下载所需的网络请求信息（例如 IP 地址），其处理方式适用 [GitHub 隐私声明](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)。已下载的曲谱可以离线使用。

演奏时，琴桥在本机读取目标窗口信息并发送模拟键盘输入。该信息用于选择演奏窗口和检查前台状态，不会发送到开发者服务器。

Microsoft Store 负责商店购买和试用许可，相关数据适用 [Microsoft 隐私声明](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement)。琴桥不处理银行卡等支付信息。

你可在软件中删除歌曲，或删除本机曲库数据库以移除本地数据。隐私问题可通过本仓库的 [Issues](https://github.com/DZXH-TX/QinBridge-Scores/issues) 联系开发者。

## English

QinBridge stores scores, instrument key mappings, and interface preferences on your device. It has no QinBridge account system, does not upload your library, key mappings, or window titles to the developer, and includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

When you open the online library, refresh its catalog, or download a score, the app requests public files from this GitHub repository. These requests do not include your local library. GitHub processes the network information needed to provide downloads, such as your IP address, under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Downloaded scores remain available offline.

During playback, QinBridge reads target-window information and sends simulated keyboard input locally. Window information is used to select the performance target and check foreground focus; it is not sent to developer servers.

Microsoft Store handles purchases and trial licenses under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). QinBridge does not process payment-card information.

You can delete songs in the app or remove the local library database to erase local data. Contact the developer about privacy through this repository's [Issues](https://github.com/DZXH-TX/QinBridge-Scores/issues).
